Support

Questions, problems or feedback about Wholehearted? Write to wholeheartedfaith.app@gmail.com. We usually answer within two business days.

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Wholehearted. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

I got a new phone. How do I get my subscription back?

Install the app, finish the setup questions and tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen, or in Settings inside the app. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Your progress, streaks and reflections are stored only on your phone, so they don't move to a new device.

I'm not getting my daily reminder.

Check that notifications for Wholehearted are on in iOS Settings, and that the daily reminder is on in the app's Settings.

I missed a day. What happens?

On the Steady pace your 40 days continue and only your streak resets. On the Strict pace the challenge starts over at day 1. You can change your pace in the app's Settings.
